Sensitivity

How far a key must go down before it sends its key. Shallower is faster but easier to trigger by accident.
With Rapid Trigger on, a key releases as soon as it moves up by this distance and presses again as soon as it moves down by it, without returning to the top first.

What these mean

Applied means the pad uses the value now. Saved means it survives unplugging: use Save to the pad on the Device page. Values that are applied but not saved are lost when the pad loses power.

Travel is an estimate. The pad measures a magnet with a Hall-effect sensor and maps the signal linearly onto the switch's nominal 4 mm of travel. Distances shown here are that estimate, in steps of 0.01 mm; they are not measured with a gauge.

The limits come from the connected pad. They are the ranges the sensing is designed for, not measured accuracy.

Calibration

Calibration measures each key at rest and fully pressed, so the pad can tell a key held while it powers up from one at rest. Keyboard output stays off while it runs.

  1. Take your hands off the pad, then start. The pad measures every key at rest for half a second.
  2. Press each key all the way down and let it go. Done keys are marked below.
  3. Finish, then save to the pad.

Backup

A backup holds the keymaps and sensitivity settings. Calibration belongs to one pad's sensors and is never restored from a file.
